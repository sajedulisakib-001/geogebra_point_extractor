GeoGebra Point Extractor

How to use this extension

Step 1 — Open your GeoGebra page.
Make sure the tab you're on actually has the GeoGebra applet loaded (the graphing tool with your points and shapes on it).

Step 2 — Click the extension icon to open this popup (you're already here!).

Step 3 — "Skipped points" box.
Type the names of any points you want to leave out, separated by commas — for example X,Y,X1,Y1. These are ignored even if they're visible on the page. Leave the default if you're not sure. Points that are hidden or disabled in GeoGebra are always skipped automatically, no need to list them here.

Step 4 — Click "Get Data."
This reads every point currently in your GeoGebra file and turns it into coordinate code, shown in the big "Output" box below. Points are grouped by shape and sorted automatically.

Step 5 — Click "Copy" to copy everything in the Output box to your clipboard, ready to paste wherever you need it.

Bonus: Color Picker.
Click "Pick Color from Screen," then click on any color anywhere on your screen (even outside GeoGebra). The hex code (like #ff5733) appears in the box and is copied to your clipboard automatically.

Tip: If nothing shows up in Output, double check you're on the right tab and that your points aren't named something the extractor doesn't recognize (it looks for names like A, B, A1, B2, C_3, etc.).
